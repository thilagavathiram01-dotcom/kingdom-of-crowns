How to play

      
    
        
  • Select: click / tap, drag a box, or double-click for same type. G workers, F army, Shift+1-4 saves a group, 1-4 recalls it
    • 
        
  • Move freely: with units selected, click / tap any open ground to move there
    • 
        
  • Fight: click / tap a visible enemy to attack; use the crosshair button + target on mobile
    • 
        
  • Workers: tap a crystal to harvest — cargo returns to HQ automatically; idle workers self-start
    • 
        
  • Fog of war: dark areas hide enemies. Scouts see far — push them forward first
    • 
        
  • Build: HQ trains Workers; Barracks trains Soldiers, Scouts, Tanks, Artillery; place Barracks + Turrets anywhere green; chain-place cheap Walls to funnel enemies (they chew through)
    • 
        
  • Win the crown: 30 kingdoms spawn far apart on a vast continent — 29 AI + you. Expect a slow ~1 hour saga: rise undisturbed, then destroy every rival HQ while AI kingdoms raid and invade each other on a slow, random basis. Peace first, then total war.
    • 
        
  • Terrain: mountains block, the river blocks — cross at the 3 lantern bridges. Forests hide ambushes; meadows grow your economy.
    • 
        
  • Camera: WASD / arrows / edge pan, wheel / pinch zoom (far out to see the continent), Q/E rotate, 2-finger drag pans, tap minimap jumps
