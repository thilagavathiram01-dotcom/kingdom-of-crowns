Defeat the Doom King

Four crowns warred over the land until the rivers ran red. Far beneath the black mountains, in the wastes men call Doomsvally, that slaughter stirred something older than any throne. Now the sky itself has begun to burn.

The Doom King has woken with an army already gathered, and it will not stop until every kingdom kneels or burns. The old laws of honour are dead. The only law left is this oath:

“One crown may fall, but all crowns will stand together.”

  1. Hold your ground — raise your walls and your army before the first wave breaks.
  2. Rally your neighbours — the other three kingdoms are your allies; you command their soldiers too.
  3. Grow strong — mine the land, forge the host, and keep Doomsvally from swallowing the world.
  4. March on Doomsvally — sound the horn, and every crown strikes as one.

Win: the Doom King is defeated.

Lose: your kingdom falls, or the Doom King destroys all the kingdoms.

How to play

        
    
          
  • Tap: select your unit / building. With units selected, tap open ground to move, enemy to attack, tree to harvest.
    • 
          
  • Drag (1 finger): pans the camera. Pinch: zoom. 2-finger drag: pan. Toggle ⤢/▦ button for box-select drag.
    • 
          
  • Command dock (right): Move / Attack / Harvest then tap a target — like a real RTS. Stop = hold position.
    • 
          
  • Bottom deck: Units tab = who is selected, Command tab = train & build. HQ trains Workers, Barracks trains the army.
    • 
          
  • Minimap: tap or drag to jump the camera across the continent.
    • 
          
  • Desktop: left-drag box-select, right-click orders, WASD/arrows pan, wheel zoom, Q/E rotate, F army, G workers, H HQ, X stop, M move order, 1-4 groups (Shift+1-4 saves).
    • 
          
  • Win: destroy all 11 rival HQs. Grow your economy, wall the bridges, then invade when your stockpiles allow.
